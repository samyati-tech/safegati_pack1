    Questions people ask before moving

    Didn't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp between 6 AM and 10 PM.

    How much advance do I pay to book?

    25% of the total confirms your booking. The rest depends on how much you're moving, how far it's going and any extra services you choose.

    Can I cancel my booking?

    Yes, within 24 hours of booking, as long as your goods haven't been dispatched. For a refund, contact customer care with your booking proof.

    Are my goods insured during the move?

    We take responsibility for your goods during the move, but we don't issue a separate insurance policy. For full cover, we recommend insuring your shipment. Loss from natural calamities, accidents, fire or riots isn't covered by us.

    How early should I book?

    3 to 4 days before your moving date, so we can plan your packing properly.

    Can you store my goods for a while?

    Yes. We store goods at our warehouse in Secunderabad, with security round the clock. Storage is charged at a lower rate; loading, unloading and local delivery are charged separately.

    Yes, between cities. What to do before handing over your car or bike
